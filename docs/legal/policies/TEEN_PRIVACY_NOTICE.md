# Teen Privacy Notice (ages 13–17) — DRAFT

> Unpublished draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. See `README.md`.

## Who can use HealthMate

People **13 and older** in the US. If you're under 13, you can't make an account. If we find out an
account belongs to someone under 13, we lock it right away and delete it after [[LAWYER: hold period;
the app currently uses 72 hours]], in case the date was typed wrong — contact [[OWNER: support email]].
Entering a different date after that won't unlock it.

## How we check your age

We ask for your **date of birth** and work out your age group (13–15, 16–17 or 18+) on our servers. We
don't ask for ID, a selfie or anything biometric. Health features stay locked until you give your date
of birth. Your age group updates by itself on your birthdays. If you later enter a date that makes you
older than we have on record, we keep the younger age group.

## Your privacy

- **Your account is yours.** HealthMate has no parent or guardian access. We don't show or send your
  conversations, records or photos to your parents, school or anyone else.
  [[LAWYER: list any legal exceptions that apply.]]
- **No ads, no selling your data** — for anyone.
- **You control the AI.** It only works if you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
- **You can delete** anything, or your whole account, in Settings.
- **We never send your date of birth to the AI** — at most your age in years.

## Sensitive topics

You can ask about things like sexual health, mental health, alcohol or drugs. The AI Health Assistant
gives general information and suggests talking to a clinician, school nurse or counselor — and, if it
might help, a parent or another adult you trust. Many health services offer confidential care for young
people; the rules depend on your state.

**If you're in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988, or call 911.** HealthMate
shows emergency guidance for messages like these straight away, without the AI.
