# HealthMate Privacy Policy — DRAFT

> **Unpublished draft for counsel review. Not legal advice. Not in force.** See `README.md`.

**Effective date:** [[OWNER: date]] · **Operator:** [[OWNER: legal name]] · **Privacy contact:** [[OWNER: email, postal address]]

This policy explains what HealthMate collects, why, who receives it, how long it's kept and what you
can do. If you live in a state with a consumer-health-data law (such as Washington or Nevada), the
**Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy** also applies and gives more detail.

## 1. Summary

- We collect what you give us and what you choose to connect (such as Apple Health).
- We use it to provide HealthMate to you. We **don't sell** it, **don't use it for ads**, and have no
  analytics or advertising trackers in the apps.
- AI features send relevant information to an AI provider only with your permission.
- You can see, export, correct and delete your data in the app.
- HealthMate is for ages 13 and up in the US. We don't knowingly keep data from anyone under 13.

## 2. What we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Email, name, sign-in method (password or Google), time zone | You |
| Age | Date of birth you enter at sign-up or later; the age band we compute from it | You |
| Profile and health record | Sex, height, goals, conditions, allergies, medications, treatment plans, care team, appointments | You |
| Health tracking | Symptoms, mood check-ins, measurements, daily summaries | You; Apple Health if you connect it |
| Conversations | Messages with the AI Health Assistant and its answers | You; AI provider |
| Reports and photos | Files you upload and their AI explanations | You; AI provider |
| Health memory | Facts saved from your conversations or records, with where they came from | You; AI suggestions you accept |
| Permissions | Which AI, report, Apple Health and voice permissions you gave or withdrew, and when | You |
| Device | Push-notification token (encrypted), app version | Your device |
| Security records | Sign-in and account events, AI usage counts and costs, safety-alert levels (no message content) | Generated by the service |

**Date of birth.** We use it to decide eligibility and age-appropriate protections. We store the age
band and its history. For ages 13–17 we also keep the date you'll turn 18, so your band updates on your
birthdays. Your profile may also hold the date of birth if you add it there. **We never send your date
of birth to an AI provider** — at most your age in whole years.

**We don't collect** government ID, selfies, face or voice prints, or precise location. [[COUNSEL:
confirm the voice feature description — speech may be processed by Apple on some devices; see the AI
disclosure.]]

## 3. How we use it

- To provide the features you use: your record, tracking, reminders, AI answers, report and photo
  explanations, health memory.
- To decide whether you can use HealthMate and apply age-appropriate protections.
- To keep HealthMate safe and working: security, preventing misuse, cost limits on AI use, and fixing
  problems (logs avoid health content).
- To meet legal obligations.

We don't use your data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models. [[COUNSEL/OWNER: confirm
against the AI provider's contract terms before publishing.]]

## 5. Your choices and rights

In the app you can:
- **see and export** your data (Settings → Privacy → Export);
- **correct** your profile, records and memories;
- **delete** individual items, your Apple Health data, or your whole account;
- **withdraw permissions** for AI, report and photo analysis, Apple Health sync and voice — this also
  stops work that was queued.

Depending on your state you may have further rights (for example to know which third parties received
your data, or to appeal a refused request). See the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy and
document 7. To make a request outside the app: [[OWNER: privacy email / web form]]. We'll verify it's
you before acting. [[COUNSEL: state-specific rights list, authorised agents, appeal process.]]

## 6. Retention

We keep your health information until you delete it or your account. Some records are kept for a
limited time after deletion — see document 7 for the exact list and periods.

## 7. Security

We use access controls on every request, database row-level security, private file storage with
short-lived links, encryption of device tokens and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly
secure; we'll notify you of a breach as the law requires.

## 8. Teens and children

HealthMate is available to people 13–17. We don't share teens' information with parents. Read the
**Teen Privacy and Age Eligibility Notice**. HealthMate isn't for children under 13 and we delete
accounts we learn belong to them.
