# HealthMate Privacy Policy — DRAFT

> Unpublished draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. See `README.md`.

**Effective date:** [[OWNER: date]] · **Operator:** [[OWNER: legal name]] · **Privacy contact:** [[OWNER: email and postal address]]

## In short

- We collect what you give us, and Apple Health data only if you connect it.
- We use it to run HealthMate for you. We **don't sell** it, **don't use it for ads**, and the apps
  contain no analytics or advertising trackers.
- AI features send relevant information to our AI provider **only if you turn them on**.
- You can see, export, correct and delete your data in the app.
- HealthMate is for people 13 and older in the US.

## 1. What we collect

Almost everything below is health information.

| What | Examples |
|---|---|
| Account | Email, name, sign-in method (password or Google), time zone |
| Age | The date of birth you give us and the age group we work out from it (13–15, 16–17 or 18+) |
| Health record | Sex, height, goals, conditions, allergies, medications, treatment plans, care team, appointments |
| Tracking | Symptoms, mood, measurements; daily summaries from Apple Health if you connect it |
| Conversations | Your messages to the AI Health Assistant and its answers |
| Reports and photos | Files you upload and their AI explanations |
| Health memory | Facts saved from your conversations or records, with where each came from |
| Permissions | Which AI, report and Apple Health permissions you gave or withdrew, and when |
| Device | An encrypted push-notification token, app version |
| Security records | Sign-in and account events, AI usage counts, emergency-alert levels — no message text |

We **don't** collect ID documents, selfies, face or voice prints, or your precise location.

**Your date of birth.** We use it to check you're old enough and to apply the right protections. We
keep your age group and its history. If you're 13–17 we also keep the date you'll turn 18, so your age
group updates on your birthdays. **We never send your date of birth to an AI provider** — at most your
age in whole years.

## 2. How we use it

- To run the features you use (your record, tracking, reminders, AI answers, report and photo
  explanations, health memory).
- To check your age and apply age-appropriate protections.
- To keep HealthMate secure and working (logs avoid health content).
- To meet legal obligations.

We don't use your data for ads, sell it, or let our AI provider train on it. [[OWNER: confirm against
the AI provider's contract before publishing.]]

## 3. Where it's stored and who receives it

Your data is stored in our database and file storage at [[OWNER: Supabase project region; hosting
provider and region]]. Files are private and only reachable through short-lived links. Every request is
checked against your account, and the database blocks access to other people's rows.

| Who | What they get | When |
|---|---|---|
| Our AI provider, [[OWNER: legal name, e.g. Anthropic, PBC]] | What's listed in the **Health Data and AI Consent Notice** | Only if you turn on AI features |
| Our database, sign-in and storage provider, [[OWNER: e.g. Supabase, Inc.]] | Everything stored in HealthMate | Always — they store it for us |
| Our hosting provider, [[OWNER: name]] | Traffic to our servers | Always — they run it for us |
| Apple | Push tokens and notification text (generic unless you turn on details); care-finder map searches; voice input when on-device recognition isn't available | If you use those features on iPhone |
| Google | Your sign-in token is checked against Google's public keys | If you sign in with Google |
| Authorities | What the law requires | Only if legally required [[LAWYER: wording]] |

No parent, guardian, school, employer or insurer can access your account through HealthMate.

## 4. Your choices

In the app you can:
- **download** your data (Settings → "Download my data");
- **correct** your profile, records and memories;
- **delete** single items, your Apple Health data or your **whole account** (Settings → "Delete account");
- **turn off** AI, report and photo analysis, and Apple Health sync. Turning one off also stops work
  that was still waiting to run.

Prefer email? Write to [[OWNER: privacy email]]. We'll confirm it's you (usually by asking you to sign
in) and reply within [[LAWYER: deadline, e.g. 45 days]]. If we say no, you can appeal at
[[OWNER: appeal contact]].

## 6. Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada and similar state laws)

This section is our consumer health data privacy policy. In those states you have the right to know
whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, to get a copy and a list of who we shared
it with, to withdraw consent, and to have it deleted. Use the in-app tools or [[OWNER: privacy email]].
We collect consumer health data only with your consent or to provide what you asked for, share it only
as in section 3, and never sell it. We don't use geofencing. [[LAWYER: confirm this section meets
Washington and Nevada requirements, or whether it must be a separate page linked from the homepage.]]

## 7. Security and breaches

We use access checks on every request, database row-level security, private file storage, encrypted
connections and encrypted device tokens. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data,
we'll tell you as the law requires.

## 8. Teens and children

People aged 13–17 can use HealthMate; see the **Teen Privacy Notice**. HealthMate isn't for children
under 13, and we delete accounts we learn belong to them.

## 9. Changes

We'll tell you in the app before important changes take effect.
