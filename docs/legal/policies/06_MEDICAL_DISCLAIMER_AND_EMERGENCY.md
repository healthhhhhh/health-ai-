## HealthMate is not medical care

HealthMate's **AI Health Assistant** gives general health information. It is not a doctor and doesn't
diagnose, treat, prescribe or replace professional care. It describes possibilities, not conclusions.
AI answers are labelled "AI-generated", show what they were based on, and can be wrong or incomplete.

- **Never start, stop, skip or change a medication or dose** because of something in HealthMate.
  HealthMate won't suggest it; ask your prescriber or a pharmacist.
- **Report and photo explanations** describe what a document or photo seems to show in plain language.
  They are not a reading by a clinician and may miss things. Your clinician's interpretation comes first.
- **Health tracking** compares your numbers with your own usual range. It doesn't tell you whether a
  value is medically significant.
- HealthMate's safety rules are a backstop, not a guarantee. [[OWNER: the triage rules are pending
  clinical review, including for teens — launch blocker.]]

## Emergencies

**Call 911** (or your local emergency number) for chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke,
severe bleeding, a seizure, severe allergic reactions, or if you think a life is at risk.

**Suicide or self-harm:** call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
**Poisoning:** Poison Help **1-800-222-1222**.

When you describe emergency symptoms, HealthMate shows fixed emergency guidance immediately. This
guidance comes from HealthMate's built-in rules on your device and on our servers — **not from the
AI** — and it works even if AI features are off. Don't wait for an answer from HealthMate in an
emergency.
