## 1. What HealthMate is

HealthMate is an AI health companion. It helps you keep your own health information in one place,
ask general questions about symptoms, understand medical reports in plain language, and track things
like symptoms, mood, sleep and steps.

HealthMate is **not a doctor, clinic, telehealth service, pharmacy or emergency service**. It doesn't
diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it never tells you to start, stop or change a medication. Using it
doesn't create a doctor–patient relationship. The **AI Medical Disclaimer** is part of these Terms.

**In an emergency, call 911. For suicide or self-harm, call or text 988.**

## 2. Who can use it

- You must be **13 or older** and in the **United States**.
- **No one under 13.** If you tell us you're under 13 at sign-up, we don't create an account. If we learn
  an existing account belongs to someone under 13, we lock it straight away and delete it.
- We ask for your date of birth before you can use health features. Please give your real date of birth.
- **If you're 13–17**, read the **Teen Privacy Notice**. [[LAWYER: whether a parent or guardian must
  also agree to these Terms for users under 18, and how.]]

## 3. Your account and your data

Keep your sign-in details private. You own the health information you add. You let us store and use
it **only to run HealthMate for you**, as the Privacy Policy explains. We don't sell it and don't use
it for ads. You can download or delete it at any time in Settings ("Download my data", "Delete account").

Only add information about **yourself** — not other people's records or photos.

## 4. AI features

AI answers can be wrong or incomplete. They're labelled "AI-generated" and show what they were based
on. Check anything important with a clinician. AI features only run if you turn them on, and you can
turn them off at any time.

## 5. Acceptable use

Don't break the law with HealthMate, try to access other people's data, disrupt the service, scrape
it, or upload intimate images (photo checks are for skin, wounds and swelling only).

## 6. Changes and ending

We may change or stop features, and we'll tell you in the app before important changes to these Terms
take effect. You can stop using HealthMate and delete your account at any time. We may close accounts
that break these Terms or the age rules.
