## 1. Consumer health data we collect

Nearly everything in HealthMate is consumer health data: conditions, allergies, medications,
symptoms, measurements, Apple Health summaries, reports and photos and their analyses, conversations
with the AI Health Assistant, health memories (including ones the AI suggested and you accepted),
safety-alert levels raised by your messages, and your age band and date of birth.

## 2. Sources

You (what you type, upload or say); Apple Health, if you connect it; the AI provider's answers and
analyses, which are stored with your record and labelled as AI-generated.

## 3. Purposes

To provide the features you ask for; to apply age eligibility and protections; to keep the service
secure; to meet legal obligations. We collect consumer health data only with your consent or as
necessary to provide the service you asked for. [[COUNSEL: map each purpose to "consent" vs
"necessary" under RCW 19.373.030 and NRS; separate consent for sharing.]]

## 5. Your rights

You can: confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data; get a copy and a list
of the third parties and affiliates we shared it with [[OWNER: the export doesn't yet include this list —
Audit G10]]; withdraw consent; and have it deleted. Exercise these in the app or at [[OWNER: privacy
contact]]. If we refuse a request you can appeal at [[OWNER: appeal contact]]; if the appeal is refused,
you may contact your state attorney general.

## 6. Minors

Teens 13–17 are consumers under these laws too. Who gives consent for a minor's consumer health data is
not settled by the statute. [[COUNSEL: whether the teen's own consent is sufficient in each state, and
whether any parental involvement is required.]] HealthMate does not give parents access to a teen's data.

## 7. Geofencing

HealthMate doesn't use geofencing. The iOS care finder runs on your device through Apple Maps; your
location isn't sent to our servers.
