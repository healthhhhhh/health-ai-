# AI Medical Disclaimer — DRAFT

> Unpublished draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. See `README.md`.

## HealthMate is not medical care

HealthMate's **AI Health Assistant** gives general health information. It isn't a doctor and doesn't
diagnose, treat or prescribe. It describes possibilities, not conclusions. AI answers are labelled
"AI-generated", show what they were based on, and **can be wrong or incomplete** — check anything
important with a clinician.

- **Medications.** Never start, stop, skip or change a medication or dose because of HealthMate.
  HealthMate won't suggest it; ask your prescriber or a pharmacist.
- **Reports and photos.** Explanations describe what a document or photo seems to show, in plain
  language. They aren't a clinician's reading and may miss things.
- **Tracking.** HealthMate compares your numbers with your own usual range. It doesn't tell you whether a
  value is medically significant.

## Emergencies

- **Call 911** for chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke, severe bleeding, a seizure, a severe
  allergic reaction, or if you think a life is at risk.
- **Suicide or self-harm:** call or text **988**.
- **Poisoning:** call **1-800-222-1222**.

When you describe emergency symptoms, HealthMate shows emergency guidance straight away. That guidance
comes from fixed safety rules on your device and our servers — **not from the AI** — and it works even
with AI features turned off. Don't wait for HealthMate in an emergency.
