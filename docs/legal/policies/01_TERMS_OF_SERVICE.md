## 1. What HealthMate is — and isn't

HealthMate is an AI health companion. It helps you organise your own health information, understand
general health topics and medical reports in plain language, and track things like symptoms, sleep
and steps. Its AI Health Assistant gives general information, not medical advice.

HealthMate is **not** a doctor, a hospital, a telehealth service, a pharmacy or an emergency service.
It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it never tells you to start, stop or change a medication
or dose. Using it does not create a clinician–patient relationship. Read the Medical Disclaimer and
Emergency Notice — it is part of these Terms.

**In an emergency, call 911.** If you're thinking about suicide or self-harm, call or text 988
(Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

## 4. Your content and data

You own the health information you add. You let us store and process it **only to provide HealthMate
to you**, as described in the Privacy Policy, the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy and the AI
disclosure. We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising. You can export and delete it.

Only upload information about **yourself**. Don't upload other people's records or photos.

on. Check anything important with a clinician. AI features only run with your permission, which you
can withdraw at any time in Settings.
